Upload a CSV and get a past / present / future forecast narrative.

Upload a CSV

Use a comma-separated file with a header row. Numeric columns are selected automatically for forecasting, anomaly checks, and the betrayal-metric flag.

How it works

  1. Choose a CSV file with at least one numeric column.
  2. Spreadsheet Prophet infers column types, likely units, and anomalies.
  3. Review the past, present, and future cards, confidence bands, and betrayal-metric flag.
  4. Export the forecast plot or result view as PDF or image.

Tips

  • Keep one row per observation and include a date or index column when possible.
  • Dense numeric columns produce stronger forecasts and clearer confidence bands.
  • Use the export controls on the result page to save the plot or full result.
